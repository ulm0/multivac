# Contract — what closing a change promises

1. Every claim it verifies is anchored somewhere that survives the archive.
2. It never writes over a record it did not create in this run.
3. The law's history — retired rows included — cannot be deleted through it.
4. Anything it silently discards, it names.
5. What it does not gate, it says: the speckit ledger and `--abandon`'s report
   are MV-110's recorded decisions, not oversights.
