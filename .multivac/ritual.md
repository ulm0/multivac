# Ritual

<!-- The closing ceremony multivac cannot check: who reviews what, who gets

- [ ] The branch is pushed and an MR is open — nothing lands on main directly.
- [ ] The MR description states the landing order and names every claim the
      change made true.
- [ ] Anything the change taught that is not yet law is either a new row or a
      line in the friction backlog — decided out loud, not left in a head.
- [ ] The site says what the tool now does: a released behavior nobody can
      read about did not ship.
